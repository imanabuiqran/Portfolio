A little bit about me

Hi, I'm Iman. I'm a creative marketer based in Dubai, with Tunisian-Palestinian-German origins.

Like my origins, my interests span far and wide. I started off my studies with a Bachelor in Communications and Media, majoring in marketing. A year in I decided to pursue a BTEC Diploma in graphic design alongside my Bachelor's. I believe the studies are intertwined not only in industry, but in its core study: storytelling. Marketing gave me the audience, the strategy, the why. Design gave me the visual literacy to speak it.

In the following two years, somewhere between my two studies; I found time to intern at Drip Media, and DFM & NASDAQ Dubai. Each experience changed my approach and understanding in some way, teaching me about how what we learn in the classroom shows up in practice.

Personally, I love playing the piano, taking pictures of funky bugs, karaoke, games that push the limits, and ranking theme parks.
