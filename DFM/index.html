DFM & NASDAQ Dubai

During my internship at DFM & NASDAQ Dubai, I worked with the marketing team through the preparations for the Capital Market Summit - one of the region's largest capital market events. Getting the hands on experience of how an event at that scale is put together was priceless, from sponsor coordination, speaker logistics, venue planning; and alongside it, I worked closely with the team's graphic designer, taking on work from a projection design for the NASDAQ tower in NYC celebrating Omniyat's first green sukuk listing, to bell-ringing ceremony screens, to assisting with designing the wallpaper decor for the CMS in Madinat Jumeirah.
